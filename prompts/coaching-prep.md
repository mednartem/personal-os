# /coaching-prep — 1:1 Readiness Cheat Sheet

## What to gather before running

1. Copy your `context/mental-model.md`
2. Copy the hub page for the person — `My Team/[Name].md` or `Stakeholders/[Name].md`
3. Copy any recent meeting notes that involved this person (last 2-4 weeks)

## Prompt — paste into Claude or Gemini

---

**CONTEXT — Mental Model:**
[paste context/mental-model.md here]

**HUB PAGE:**
[paste the person's hub page here]

**RECENT MEETING NOTES:**
[paste relevant recent meeting notes here]

---

Prepare me for a 1:1 or coaching conversation with this person. I want to skip the catch-up phase and go straight to substance.

Produce a cheat sheet in this format:

**Context in 30 Seconds**
[Who is this person, what do they own, what's their current situation]

**Open Action Items (from them)**
[What did they commit to? What's the status?]

**Open Action Items (from me)**
[What did I commit to? Have I delivered?]

**Recent Wins**
[What have they done well recently that I should acknowledge?]

**Coaching Themes**
[What are we working on together? What growth areas are relevant right now?]

**Suggested Questions**
[3-5 questions to ask that go deeper than status updates — aim for questions that reveal how they think, not just what they did]

**Watch For**
[Based on the context, what patterns or dynamics should I be attentive to in this conversation?]

---

## Output

Read in chat — no file to save. Use it as a pre-meeting reference.
