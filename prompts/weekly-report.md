# /weekly-report — Weekly Synthesis

## What to gather before running

1. Copy your `Goals.md`
2. Copy all daily reflection files from this week — `Reflections/Daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (paste them all)

## Prompt — paste into Claude or Gemini

---

**GOALS:**
[paste Goals.md here]

**THIS WEEK'S DAILY REFLECTIONS:**
[paste all daily reflection files from this week here]

---

Synthesize this week into a structured weekly report. Your job is to surface patterns and honest assessment, not just summarize what happened.

Produce a report in this format:

```markdown
---
type: reflection
horizon: weekly
week: [YYYY-Wnn]
date: [Friday's date]
tags: [#type/reflection]
---

# Weekly Report — Week [n], [date range]

## Highlights
[2-3 genuine wins or important moments this week]

## Progress vs. Goals
[For each annual goal: what moved? what stalled? be direct]

## Patterns Observed
[What recurring themes, friction points, or dynamics showed up this week?]

## Blockers / Risks
[What is stuck or at risk? What needs attention next week?]

## Next Week's Priority
[Single most important thing — not a list, one thing]

## Metrics (if applicable)
[Any numbers worth tracking: time allocation, tasks completed, etc.]
```

Be direct and specific. If a goal got no attention this week, say so. If the same blocker appeared three days in a row, name it.
