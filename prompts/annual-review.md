# /annual-review — Annual Review

## What to gather before running

1. Copy your `Goals.md`
2. Copy all monthly synthesis files from this year — `Reflections/Monthly/YYYY-MM.md` (paste them all)
3. Optional: copy any annual reflection from last year if you want to track continuity

## Prompt — paste into Claude or Gemini

---

**GOALS:**
[paste Goals.md here]

**THIS YEAR'S MONTHLY SYNTHESES:**
[paste all monthly synthesis files from this year here]

**LAST YEAR'S ANNUAL REVIEW (optional):**
[paste if available]

---

Synthesize this year into a strategic annual review. This is the highest-level reflection — look for patterns and truths that only become visible across a full year.

Produce a review in this format:

```markdown
---
type: reflection
horizon: annual
year: [YYYY]
tags: [#type/reflection]
---

# Annual Review — [Year]

## Year in One Sentence
[The single most important thing that happened or changed this year]

## Progress vs. Annual Goals
[Final honest tally per goal — did you achieve it? Why or why not?]

## Themes That Defined the Year
[What was this year really about? What patterns ran through every month?]

## What Grew
[Skills, relationships, capabilities, clarity — what genuinely improved?]

## What Didn't Work
[Goals missed, patterns that persisted, things that need to change — be honest]

## What I'd Do Differently
[Specific, not vague — if you could replay the year, what changes?]

## Heading Into Next Year
[Top 1-2 priorities for the coming year and why they matter now]
```

Be direct. This is not a highlight reel — it's a genuine account of the year.
