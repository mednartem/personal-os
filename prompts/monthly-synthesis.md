# /monthly-synthesis — Monthly Pattern Analysis

## What to gather before running

1. Copy your `Goals.md`
2. Copy all weekly reports from this month — `Reflections/Weekly/` (paste them all)

## Prompt — paste into Claude or Gemini

---

**GOALS:**
[paste Goals.md here]

**THIS MONTH'S WEEKLY REPORTS:**
[paste all weekly report files from this month here]

---

Synthesize this month into a strategic monthly review. Look for patterns that are invisible week-to-week but clear across the full month.

Produce a synthesis in this format:

```markdown
---
type: reflection
horizon: monthly
month: [YYYY-MM]
tags: [#type/reflection]
---

# Monthly Synthesis — [Month Year]

## Month in One Sentence
[The single most important thing that happened or changed this month]

## Progress vs. Annual Goals
[Honest assessment per goal — are you on track for the year?]

## Themes That Emerged
[What patterns showed up repeatedly? What was the month really about?]

## Time Allocation Reality Check
[Where did time actually go vs. where it should have gone?]

## What Needs to Change
[One or two specific behaviors or priorities to shift next month]

## Wins Worth Remembering
[Things to feel good about — don't skip this]

## Heading Into Next Month
[Top priority for the next 4 weeks]
```

Be strategic, not just descriptive. The goal is insight, not a summary.
