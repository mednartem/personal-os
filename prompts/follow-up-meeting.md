# /follow-up-meeting — Draft Calendar Invite

## What to gather before running

1. Copy the processed meeting note from `Meeting Notes/`
2. Know your general availability for the next 2 weeks (or paste your calendar if you want specific slots)

## Prompt — paste into Claude or Gemini

---

**MEETING NOTE:**
[paste the processed meeting note here]

**MY AVAILABILITY (optional):**
[paste calendar or describe availability — e.g., "free most mornings, busy Thursday"]

---

Based on this meeting note, draft a calendar invite for the follow-up meeting.

1. First, identify from the note: what follow-up was agreed to? What needs to be discussed next time?
2. Suggest a meeting duration (default 30 min unless the context suggests otherwise)
3. Draft the calendar invite:

```
Title: [descriptive title — not just "Follow-up"]
Duration: [X minutes]
Attendees: [names from the note]

Agenda:
1. [item from open questions or next steps]
2. [item]
3. [item]

Context:
[1-2 sentences linking this to the previous meeting]
```

If I provided availability, suggest 2-3 specific time options. If not, flag that I need to check my calendar.

---

## Output

Copy from chat → paste into calendar invite.
