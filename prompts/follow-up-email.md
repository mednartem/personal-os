# /follow-up-email — Draft Meeting Recap Email

## What to gather before running

1. Copy your `context/mental-model.md` (especially the Writing Style and Communication Style sections)
2. Copy the processed meeting note from `Meeting Notes/`

## Prompt — paste into Claude or Gemini

---

**CONTEXT — Mental Model:**
[paste context/mental-model.md here]

**MEETING NOTE:**
[paste the processed meeting note here]

---

Draft a professional meeting recap email based on this meeting note. The email should:

- Match my writing and communication style from the context above
- Be sent to the meeting participants (use names from the note)
- Open with a 1-2 sentence tl;dr of the meeting
- List key decisions clearly (what was decided, by whom)
- List action items with owners and due dates
- Close with next steps or the next touchpoint if known

Format:

```
To: [participants]
Subject: Recap: [meeting title] — [date]

[tl;dr]

**Decisions**
- 

**Action Items**
- [ ] Owner — item — due date

**Next Steps**
[what happens next]

[sign-off]
```

Keep it under 200 words. Busy people scan, not read.

---

## Output

Copy from chat → paste into your email client.
