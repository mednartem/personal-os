# Goals

## Work Goals

1. Automate 80% of regression suite by Q3 (from 35% today)
2. Reduce release cycle from 3 weeks to 1 week
3. Establish quality culture — developers own quality, QA enables it
4. Test strategy document adopted across all 3 product teams

## Personal Goals

1. Launch a small side project — something shipped and used by at least 10 people
2. Improve sleep consistency — in bed by 11pm at least 5 nights a week
3. Take one proper holiday this year, fully offline

## Quarterly Focus

**Q1:** Automation framework selected and running, first 200 tests written, test strategy v1 drafted and in review

**Q2:** Automation at 60%, release cycle at 2 weeks, test strategy ratified

**Q3:** Automation at 80%, release cycle at 1 week, quality culture metrics baseline established

**Q4:** Sustain, measure cultural shift, document what worked

## Personal Development

**Working on:** Risk prioritization — getting better at distinguishing real blockers from theoretical risks

**How I'll know I'm improving:** I approve a release with known low-severity issues at least twice without escalating unnecessarily

## North Star

A year where quality is a team habit, not a gate. Releases are faster because quality was built in, not added at the end.
