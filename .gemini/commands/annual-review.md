Read Goals.md and all monthly synthesis files from this year in Reflections/Monthly/.

Synthesize them into a strategic annual review. This is the highest-level reflection — look for patterns and truths that only become visible across a full year. Be direct, not a highlight reel.

Write the review to Reflections/Annual/[YYYY].md:

```markdown
---
type: reflection
horizon: annual
year: [YYYY]
tags: [#type/reflection]
---

# Annual Review — [Year]

## Year in One Sentence
[The single most important thing that happened or changed this year]

## Progress vs. Annual Goals
[Final honest tally per goal — did you achieve it? Why or why not?]

## Themes That Defined the Year
[What was this year really about? What patterns ran through every month?]

## What Grew
[Skills, relationships, capabilities, clarity — what genuinely improved?]

## What Didn't Work
[Goals missed, patterns that persisted, things that need to change — be honest]

## What I'd Do Differently
[Specific, not vague — if you could replay the year, what changes?]

## Heading Into Next Year
[Top 1-2 priorities for the coming year and why they matter now]
```

If last year's annual review exists in Reflections/Annual/, read it too for continuity.
