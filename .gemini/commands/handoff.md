Help me create a handoff note before I stop working.

Ask me these questions one at a time, waiting for my answer each time:

1. What were you just working on? (Be specific.)
2. Where exactly did you leave off — what is the current state?
3. What is the single first action to take when you return?
4. Any open questions or blockers to be aware of?

Then write the note to Handoffs/[today's date]-[time].md:

```markdown
---
type: handoff
date: [date and time]
tags: [#type/handoff]
---

# Handoff — [date and time]

## What I was working on

## Where I left off

## Open questions / blockers

## First step when I return
```

Keep it short — scannable in 30 seconds.
