Ask me to paste the document I want to review.

After I paste it, provide high-quality structured feedback. Use my context to ensure the feedback aligns with my priorities, technical standards, and leadership principles.

Write the feedback to Docs/feedback-[doc-title].md:

```markdown
---
type: doc
date: [today]
tags: [#type/doc]
---

# Feedback: [Document Title]

## Strengths
[What works well? Be specific — name what the author did right and why it matters]

## Critical Gaps
[What is missing, unclear, or wrong? These must be addressed before the document is ready]

## Suggestions
[Improvements that would make this stronger, but aren't blockers — specific and actionable]

## Key Question
[The one question to ask the author that gets to the heart of what this document is really deciding]
```

Rules:
- Be direct and specific — vague feedback is useless
- Distinguish between "this is wrong" and "this is a style preference"
- Match the feedback depth to the document's importance
- If the document touches a program or initiative in my context, bring that perspective in
