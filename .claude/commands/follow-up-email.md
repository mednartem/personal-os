Read context/mental-model.md from this vault (especially the Writing Style and Communication Style sections).

Ask me which meeting note to use, or ask me to paste the meeting note.

After I provide it, draft a professional meeting recap email. The email should:

- Match my writing and communication style from my mental model
- Be sent to the meeting participants (use names from the note)
- Open with a 1-2 sentence tl;dr of the meeting
- List key decisions clearly (what was decided, by whom)
- List action items with owners and due dates
- Close with next steps or the next touchpoint if known

Format:

```
To: [participants]
Subject: Recap: [meeting title] — [date]

[tl;dr]

**Decisions**
- 

**Action Items**
- [ ] Owner — item — due date

**Next Steps**
[what happens next]

[sign-off]
```

Keep it under 200 words. Busy people scan, not read.

Output in chat — copy into your email client.
